# 🏴‍☠️ Pastry Pirates *on the Sugar Seas*

## The Story

You sailed all the way from London to Barbados with one dream: to become the **Best Baker in the Caribbean**. There's just one problem — your pantry is empty!

The ingredients you need are scattered across the islands of the Sugar Seas: wheat on one, eggs on another, cocoa far to the south... and there's no shop that sells them. If you want to bake, you'll have to **sail out and get them yourself**.

But beware, matey: every other baker in Barbados had the same idea, and out on these waters, bakers turn into **pirates**. It's plunder or be plundered!

## The Goal

Collect **every ingredient on your recipe map**, then sail home and dock at Barbados. The first baker home fires up their ovens and wins!

## Setting Sail

Each captain gets a **recipe map** (5 ingredients), **3 gold coins**, and a ship docked at Barbados, in the middle of the sea. Each island holds only a few **crates** of its ingredient — when they're gone, they're gone. The player who looks most like a pirate goes first (after that, the first turn passes around the table each round).

**Keep your recipe map to yourself.** It's secret — don't show or tell rivals which ingredients you're after. Crates in your ship's hold, on the other hand, are fair game for anyone to see: every captain's cargo is out in the open.

## Your Turn

Every turn has three parts: **Wind → Sail → Act.**

### 1. 🌬️ Wind

Spin for the wind at the start of each round — every ship drifts **1 square** that direction (**2 squares** in a storm!).

- **Moored ships are safe.** If you docked last turn, are tied up at a berth, or are at Barbados, the wind can't push you into land.
- Otherwise, if the wind would smash you into an island: **pay 1 coin to dodge**, or flip the bullion — **heads** you drop anchor safely, **tails** you run aground and lose *half your coins* in repairs!
- The wind can never blow you onto a dock that's already full.

### 2. ⛵ Sail

Pay **1 coin** to sail up to **3 squares** (north, south, east, or west). You may sail *past* other ships, but you can't end your move on top of one. No coins? You drift with the wind — better go fishing!

### 3. ⚓ Act (choose one)

**Dock** — Tie up at an island's dock (one ship at a time!) and flip the bullion:
- **Heads:** take a crate of that island's ingredient!
- **Tails:** take 3 coins — *or* pay 3 coins to buy the crate anyway.
- One flip per visit. Want another? Sail away and come back.
- **Ship's hold:** you may take crates that *aren't* on your recipe. They make excellent trade bait...

**Attack** — Pick a fight with an adjacent ship! Pay **2 coins** for powder, then take turns flipping the bullion:
- Heads beats tails: **1 point**. Both heads cancel. Both tails, nothing.
- **Fire the broadside!** Once per battle, the attacker may pay 1 coin to reflip one of their tails.
- First to **3 points** wins. The loser hands over a crate (winner picks which) or 5 coins — loser's choice. If they can't pay in full, the winner takes everything they've got.
- Then the two ships **swap places**!

**Parley** — Hail *any* captain, anywhere on the sea, and make a deal: crates, coins, promises — anything you can negotiate. When a trade is struck, **both** captains collect 1 bonus coin from the bank (the harbor tax refund).

**Fish** — Flip the bullion: **heads** nets 2 coins, **tails**... just a sardine (worth 1 coin).

**Start your bakery!** — At Barbados with your whole recipe? Declare victory and fire up the ovens!

## 🌀 The Trade Winds

The open sea around the rim of the world flows in a mighty **clockwise current**. Sail (or get blown) into it, and you're instantly swept to the far corner of that stretch — a daring shortcut for captains in a hurry!

## Winning

The moment the first baker docks at Barbados with a full recipe, every other captain gets **one last turn** to make it home too. If more than one baker makes it, the ovens get hot: **BAKEOFF!** Flip head-to-head, first to 5 points — winner takes the crown. 👑

*Best Baker in the Caribbean. May your flips be heads and your rivals be broke.*
