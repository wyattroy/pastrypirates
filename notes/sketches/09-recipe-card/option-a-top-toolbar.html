Spiced Cocoa Shortbread

Buttery, melt-in-your-mouth shortbread biscuits infused with warm cinnamon and rich cocoa.
Yield
About 24 cookies
Ingredients
  • 225 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 100 g granulated sugar, plus 2 tbsp extra for rolling
  • 2 tbsp whole milk
  • 250 g all-purpose flour
  • 40 g unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon, plus 1 tsp extra for rolling
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • 100 g dark chocolate, chopped (optional, for dipping)
Steps
  1. Cream the butter and sugar together for 2–3 minutes until pale and fluffy. Beat in the milk.
  2. Whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, cinnamon and salt, then add to the butter mixture and mix on low until just combined.
  3. Shape into a log, chill for at least 2 hours.
  4. Preheat oven to 165°C. Slice into rounds, roll edges in cinnamon-sugar.
  5. Bake 15–18 minutes until set but not browned. Cool completely.
  6. Optional: dip half of each cookie in melted chocolate.
