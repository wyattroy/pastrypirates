🏴‍☠️ Pastry Pirates on the Sugar Seas
You left London's fog for the sunny shores of Tortuga to start a bakery -- but you have no ingredients! Out here, it's plunder or be plundered: so sail the Sugar Seas to gather rare ingredients from the archipelago, out-bargain and out-battle rival captains, and race home to be crowned Best Baker in the Caribbean.

📖 How to play

🎯 The goal

Collect every ingredient on your recipe map, then sail home and dock at the Isle of Tortuga. First baker home wins!

⚓ Setting sail

Each captain gets a recipe map (5 ingredients), 3 gold coins, and a ship docked at the Isle of Tortuga. Each island holds only a few crates — when they're gone, they're gone.

Your turn: wind → sail → act

🌬️ Wind. Every ship drifts 1 square with the wind (2 in a storm!). Moored ships are safe — if you docked last turn, sit at a berth, or are at the Isle of Tortuga, the wind can't push you into land. Otherwise, blown at an island you must pay 1🌕 to dodge or flip: heads drop anchor safely, tails run aground and lose half your coins! The wind never blows you onto a full dock.

⛵ Sail. Pay 1🌕 to move up to 3 squares (N/S/E/W). Sail past other ships, but don't end on one. No coins? You drift — better go fishing!

⚓ Act — choose one:

Dock — tie up at an island's dock (one ship at a time!) and flip: heads take a crate; tails take 3🌕 — or pay 3🌕 to buy the crate anyway. Stay as long as you like and keep flipping. Ship's hold: you may take crates that aren't on your recipe — they make excellent trade bait...

Attack — fight an adjacent ship! Pay 2🌕 powder, then flip head-to-head: heads beats tails (1 point), both heads cancel. Fire the broadside! Once per battle the attacker may pay 1🌕 to reflip one tails. Whoever's shooting downwind also gets one free reflip. Defend two heads in a row and you may pay 1🌕 to flee to safety instead of fighting on. First to 2 points wins; loser hands over a crate (winner picks) or 5🌕 — loser's choice. Can't pay in full? Winner takes everything. Win as the attacker and you seize the loser's square (ships swap places); if the attack is repelled, no one moves. Spectators can call the winner for a free dubloon at the Lookout's Call — or back their call with coin for double or nothing.

Parley — hail any captain, anywhere, and make a deal: crates, coins, or both together. When a trade is struck, both captains collect +1🌕 from the bank.

Fish — flip: heads nets 2🌕, tails... just a sardine (1🌕).

Start your bakery! — at the Isle of Tortuga with your whole recipe? Declare victory! 🧁

🌀 The trade winds

The sea around the rim of the world flows in a mighty clockwise current, in arcs of varying length each game. Sail (or get blown) into it and you're instantly swept to the far corner of that stretch — a daring shortcut for captains in a hurry!

⛈️ Storms

Roughly 1 in 10 rounds brews a storm: the wind spins and pushes everyone 2 squares, then spins again and pushes 2 more — you can still pay to dodge an island on either leg.

⏰ The shot clock

Take too long on your turn and you'll lose a random ingredient from your hold (or 5🌕 if you're out of ingredients) and miss the turn.

👑 Winning

When the first baker docks home, everyone else gets one last turn. If more than one makes it: BAKEOFF! Flip head-to-head, first to 3 points takes the crown.

May your flips be heads and your rivals be broke.
