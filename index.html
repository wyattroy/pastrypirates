How to play

Collect every ingredient on your recipe map (the chips by your name), then sail home to ⚓ Barbados in the center and dock to win.

Each turn: the wind drifts you 1 square (2 in a storm) — moored ships are safe. Then you may sail up to 3 squares for 1🌕. Then act: dock at an island to flip for a crate, attack an adjacent ship, parley (trade) with anyone, or fish for coins.

Watch the yellow prompt panel by the captains list — when it's your turn, your choices appear there. The 🌀 trade winds around the rim sweep you clockwise around the world for a fast shortcut.
