# The CEO brief — hand this shape to every CEO review

**Rule 25.** After work, before Wyatt sees it: a fresh agent judges whether **the ask was executed**.

## The three things it must be given

1. **His request, VERBATIM.** Not a summary. The summary is where the drift already happened.
2. **What was actually done** — files, commits, measurements, and what was *not* done.
3. **The previous CEO's verdict**, so it can say whether the same fault is recurring.

## The question it answers

**Did the thing he asked for happen?** — not "is this good work".

**Adjacent, competent, impressive work that misses the ask is precisely what this exists to catch.**
That is the documented failure mode: 22 fixes shipped, 4 verified, everything true, the ask missed.

## The template

> You are the CEO. Repo: /Users/wyattroy/Documents/Projects/pastrypirates. READ-ONLY — do not edit,
> create or commit. Absolute paths.
>
> **WYATT ASKED, VERBATIM:**
> "…"
>
> **WHAT WAS DONE, as claimed:**
> … (with commits, files and measurements)
>
> **WHAT WAS NOT DONE, as admitted:**
> …
>
> **THE PREVIOUS CEO'S VERDICT:**
> …
>
> **ANSWER, in this order:**
> 1. For EACH thing he asked for: DONE / PARTIAL / NOT DONE, with the evidence you checked.
> 2. What was delivered that he did **not** ask for, and whether it displaced something he did.
> 3. Is any claim above unsupported by what is actually in the repo? Name it.
> 4. Is the fault from the last verdict fixed, or has it recurred in new clothing?
> 5. One sentence Wyatt should read first.
>
> **RULES:** Plain English — he is a founder and designer, not an engineer; define any professional
> term once, in the same sentence. **You may say NO.** A criticism with no file:line citation is an
> opinion, not a finding. Assume the author is flattering himself. Do not start a browser or server.
> Bound your effort.

## What makes it rot

**A kind paraphrase.** His verdict reaches Wyatt in ITS words, especially when bad — the paraphraser
is the one with the motive to soften it. The first CEO review opened with *"The unit shipped a
process today and its very first output is a lie."* That sentence is the product.
