# Pastry Pirates — the laser-cut set

Three complete versions of a physical Pastry Pirates, as vector files ready for Rhino (SVG and
DXF), in black and white, two layers: **CUT** (red hairline) and **RASTER** (black fill, engrave).

**Start here:** open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. It shows every piece in all three
versions side by side, lets you tick the one you want per piece, leave notes, and copy the lot
as one block of text to hand back.

Everything is generated by [`generate.mjs`](generate.mjs) from the game's own numbers — the 15×15
grid, the 177-square round sea, the 40-square trade-wind ring that flows clockwise, Tortuga in
the centre with its four berths, the seven island footprints and the seven ingredients — so
the board is the board people already play on, not a redrawing of it.

## The three versions

| | V1 · The Plank | V2 · The Pixel World | V3 · The Round Table |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board | Square plank, 348 mm, round world engraved; wind dial engraved into a corner with its pivot hole cut; compass rose; title | Cut along the stepped edge of the sea itself, ~314 mm | True circle, 330 mm, double ring at the water's edge |

| Crates | Rounded squares | Round, icon knocked out of a black badge | Hexagons |
| Whirlpools | Spiral line | Broken rings | Bold spiral with an eye |
| Wind spinner | 4 quadrants, a storm wedge in each (20%) | 20-sector weather wheel, 4 storm sectors | Plain 4-quadrant dial + a separate 5-sector storm spinner |
| Ships | Standing sloop + base | Standing galleon + base | Flat disc tokens |

The ingredient icons are shared across the three — a wheat ear, a milk bottle, sugar cubes, a
speckled egg, a chocolate bar, cinnamon sticks, a vanilla flower — redrawn from the app's art as
cuttable outlines. Only the framing changes.

All sizes assume **20 mm squares and 3 mm material**. Both are one flag away:

```bash
node physical-board/generate.mjs --cell 24 --material 3.2 --sheet 400
```

## Opening in Rhino

- **SVG**: `Import`, units are millimetres (the file declares `width="…mm"`), every colour is
  already a layer-named group: `CUT` and `RASTER`. Rhino 7+ reads the arcs as arcs.
- **DXF**: R12 flavour, two layers `CUT` (colour 1) and `RASTER` (colour 7). Curves are
  polylines. Set document units to millimetres if Rhino asks.
- Red hairline = cut. Black fill = engrave. To send RASTER to a vector-engrave instead, keep the
  same layer and change the operation in your laser software — nothing in the file needs to move.
- **No kerf compensation is applied.** Tokens are cut 0.4 mm inside their grid square on every
  side, so they drop in freely after the kerf; the ship tab/slot and the V2 nub/socket are given
  0.15–0.3 mm of clearance and may want a test cut on your material.

## Assembly

**Wind spinner.** Stack from the bottom: dial → hollow forecast arrow → washer → NOW arrow →
washer → M3 × 12 bolt and nyloc nut (or a brass paper fastener). On V1 the dial is also engraved
into the board's corner with its hole cut, so the arrows can mount straight onto the board.

**Docks.** V1: glue the plank strip on top of the dock square, flush with the edge that faces the
island — the overhang rests on the island piece. V2: push the nub into any socket. V3: butt the
dock against the island so the slots line up and drop the post through; it stands up like a
bollard.

**Ships (V1, V2).** The tab under the hull drops into the base slot.

## How the physical rules differ from the app

- **Whirlpools.** The app splits the ring into four arcs of random length; a ship swept into the
  ring is carried to the end of its arc. On the table: put the four whirlpool tiles on any four
  ring squares (the ask was "randomly each turn"); a ship that enters the ring is carried
  clockwise to the next whirlpool.
- **Wind and forecast.** Two arrows on one dial. At the start of a round, turn NOW to where the
  forecast arrow points, then spin the forecast. The app hides a coming storm's direction: when
  the forecast lands in a storm wedge, put the storm cloud on the board and leave the forecast
  arrow alone; when that round begins, spin NOW itself — the storm blows that way, three squares,
  every ship. (V3 does this with its separate storm spinner instead of wedges.)
- **Island markers.** The app deals shapes and ingredients at random each game and engraves
  nothing, so the marker disc is how a shape becomes "the cinnamon island" for one voyage.

## What is deliberately not decided yet

- **Square size.** 20 mm squares make V1 a 348 mm plank, which is too wide for an 11" × 19.5"
  Glowforge bed but fine on a 600 × 400 bed. Tell me the bed and I regenerate.
- **Kerf.** Not compensated — see above.
- **Colour.** Everything is wood-and-black. The four captains are told apart by sail pattern;
  paint if you want the app's pink/teal/green/orange.
