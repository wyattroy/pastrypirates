# Pastry Pirates — the laser-cut set

A physical Pastry Pirates as vector files ready for Rhino (SVG and DXF), black and white, two
layers: **CUT** (red hairline) and **RASTER** (black fill, engrave).

**This folder lives on branch `physical-board` and never on `main`** (Wyatt, 2026-08-22: players
of the digital game should not see this work). On `main` the folder is git-ignored.

**Start here:** open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. Every piece, the assembled board,
and the cutting sheets; tick "approved" per group, leave notes, copy the lot as one block of text.

Everything is generated by [`generate.mjs`](generate.mjs) from the game's own numbers — the 15×15
grid, the 177-square round sea, the 40-square trade-wind ring flowing clockwise, Tortuga in the
centre with its four berths, the seven island footprints and the seven ingredients — so the board
is the board people already play on.

## The current design: V3 · The Round Table, 25 mm squares, 6 mm material

Chosen 2026-08-22 from three versions (the other two, V1 "Plank" and V2 "Pixel World", are in git
history and can be regenerated with `--versions v1,v2`).

- **Board Ø 409 mm** — Scrabble-sized (Scrabble is 381 mm; Monopoly 508 mm).
- **Cut in FIVE pieces:** four jigsaw quadrants that lock to each other with three puzzle knobs
  per seam, and **Tortuga as the fifth piece** — a 24.9 mm square that drops into the hole the four
  quadrants leave in the middle, locking their inner corners. The four quadrants have the **same
  cut outline** (one shape rotated four times), so they are interchangeable and nest identically;
  only the engraving differs.
- **Seams run along grid lines** — a pinwheel around Tortuga's square (x = 200 and y = 175 and
  their rotations), so every square, berths and rim included, is whole on one piece. Knobs sit
  mid-square, two squares apart, each inside a single square of the neighbouring piece. A grid
  line that crosses a knob is engraved onto that knob, so the grid reads continuous once locked.
- Rim marks are the app's own wind chevron (`assets/wind-arrow.png`), one per ring square,
  rotated to the ring's clockwise tangent exactly as `buildRimFlow()` does.
- **Kerf 0.18 mm**, compensated on the cutting sheets only: every cut line is offset 0.09 mm away
  from the wood that stays (outward on outlines, inward on holes), so pieces come out true size.
  Knob-to-socket play is 0.1 mm in total after compensation — a firm slide fit.
- **600 × 400 mm bed**, 6 mm margin: everything fits on **two sheets**.

| `islands`, `docks`, `crates`, `markers`, `whirlpools`, `spinner`, `ships`, `recipes`, `extras` | Each group of pieces on its own, for review | No — design views, no kerf |

Piece count: 4 quadrants + Tortuga, 7 islands, 7 docks + 7 mooring posts, 28 crates (four per
ingredient: three to stock an island, one black-market spare), 7 island markers, 4 whirlpool tiles,
wind dial + NOW arrow + forecast arrow + 2 washers, storm spinner + its arrow, 4 ship discs, 21
recipe cards (every 5-of-7 recipe exactly once), storm cloud, first-player wheel, rules card.

## Regenerating

```bash
node physical-board/generate.mjs                                   # 25 mm, 6 mm, kerf 0.18, 600x400, V3
node physical-board/generate.mjs --cell 30 --kerf 0.2 --bedw 900 --bedh 600
node physical-board/generate.mjs --versions v1,v2,v3 --cell 20 --material 3   # the original three
```

## Opening in Rhino

- **SVG**: `Import`; units are millimetres (`width="…mm"`); `CUT` and `RASTER` are layer-named
  groups. Arcs come in as arcs except on the cutting sheets, where kerf-offset outlines are
  polylines.
- **DXF**: R12, layers `CUT` (colour 1) and `RASTER` (colour 7), polylines. Set units to mm.
- Red hairline = cut. Black fill = engrave. To send RASTER to a vector-engrave instead, keep the
  layer and change the operation in your laser software.

## Assembly

**Board.** Lock the four quadrants (any quadrant fits any position — match the engraving), then
press Tortuga into the centre hole last.

**Wind spinner.** Bottom to top: dial → hollow forecast arrow → washer → NOW arrow → washer. In
6 mm that is 30 mm of wood: an M3 × 38 bolt with a nyloc nut, or a long brass paper fastener.

**Docks.** Butt the dock against any island edge so the two slots line up, drop the mooring post
through; it stands up like a bollard and locks the pair.

## How the physical rules differ from the app

- **Whirlpools.** The app splits the ring into four arcs of random length. On the table: put the
  four whirlpool tiles on any four ring squares; a ship that enters the ring is carried clockwise
  to the next whirlpool.
- **Wind and forecast.** At the start of a round, turn NOW to where the forecast arrow points, then
  spin the forecast. Spin the storm spinner too: if it lands on the storm sector, put the cloud on
  the board and leave the forecast arrow alone; when that round begins, spin NOW itself — the storm
  blows that way, three squares, every ship (the app hides a coming storm's direction too).
- **Island markers.** Shapes and ingredients are dealt fresh each game, so the marker disc is how a
  shape becomes "the cinnamon island" for one voyage.
