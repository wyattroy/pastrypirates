*Why not the obvious alternative.* A `rules.html` that `fetch()`es `index.html` at load and injects

modal fills its spans live because a 2-player table has different crate prices from a 4-player one

default — which means the honest thing is to generate it from `rulesFacts` at publish time and let
the gate re-derive it, not to ship empty spans that render blank to a crawler.
