**Done — your recipe art is WebP, and the game is half a megabyte lighter to open.** All 21
pictures converted, not one pixel resized, `/classic` sharing the same files as you chose. The art
folder is 10.05 MB, down from 17.79 MB when you first raised this.

**And here is the answer to the question you asked alongside "do it" — you were right to be
surprised.** The picture in a recipe card is capped at **220 pixels tall** by one line of CSS, on
every device including your laptop, so it is never drawn wider than about **290 layout pixels**.
What changes between devices is only how many real screen dots go into those 290: 290 on an old
screen, 580 on a 2× phone, and **879 on your 3× phone**. The files are 512 wide. So on your own
phone the art is being stretched about 1.7× — that is exactly why it reads as already too small,
and it is why shrinking these files would have made them visibly softer.

**One thing nobody has ever looked at, so I am telling you rather than guessing:** the recipe card
has a Download-PDF button, and printing asks about three times as much of the same picture as a
screen does. That is where this art is thinnest. No test in the project has ever photographed it.
