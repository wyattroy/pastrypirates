maximum pixel size in the real gameplay."* **I did not resize anything, and the CEO said that was
not good enough. It is right, so the item stays open.**

**What I built:** a probe that plays the game at three screen sizes and, for every one of the 149
pictures, reports how big it actually gets drawn versus how many pixels the file carries. The
answer is now written down — `.planning/ASSET-DISPLAY-SIZES.md` — instead of living in a terminal
somebody has to re-run.

**Where I went wrong, twice, on the same number.** How big art needs to be depends on how far the
game can zoom in. First I used the *director's* zoom limit — that said the island art was twice as
big as it needs to be, and the obvious next step was to halve your commissioned artwork. Then I
found players can pinch further than the director ever zooms, which flipped it to "nothing is
oversized" — but I'd credited a *laptop* with a two-finger pinch it can't make. Corrected, the
board art sits right on the line: not clearly wasteful, not clearly safe.

**The honest size: about 1.5–2 MB of 10.7 MB is still recoverable — the game could get roughly
15–20% lighter.** Not the whole thing, and not nothing.
