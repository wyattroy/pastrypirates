**Opened two of the ten covered screenshots by eye (rule 19/22), not just the numbers:**

**⚠ A SEPARATE ANOMALY, NOT YET UNDERSTOOD, NAMED SO THE NEXT READER DOESN'T RE-DISCOVER IT COLD:**
every single one of the 42 poses read `STILL MOVING at the 10s cap` — the instrument's own
settle-detector (3 consecutive identical signatures over up to 10s, 200ms polls) never once fired,
on any pose, at any size. I do not have a confirmed cause. Two honest candidates, neither traced:
(a) something in the fan/pill placement loop keeps nudging a rect by a sub-quantization amount
forever (the signature rounds to `/8` px buckets — a value oscillating across a bucket edge would
never repeat), or (b) a genuine continuous re-layout (camera drift, an idle boat-bob, or a rAF loop
touching `.apBtn`/`.apMsg` positions every frame). **This does NOT appear to invalidate the covered
count**: `covered` is read once at the 10s mark regardless of settle state, and the SAME 5 positions
hit on BOTH scenarios (independently posed, independently timed) — if the 10s-mark read were an
arbitrary phase of some oscillation, I would expect scenario-to-scenario disagreement at a given
position, and there is none. But it means "settled" cannot be claimed for these screenshots in the
strict sense the instrument's own name promises, and it is a live, real, unexplained deviation from
every sibling instrument's behaviour (`w54_call_clear_of_ask.mjs` settles normally on the same kind
of prompt). Filed as its own open question, not swept under this row's finding.

**MECHANISM — TRACED, NOT YET FIX-CONFIRMED.** Read `src/ui/stage.js`'s radial-fan placement
function in full (~2955–3860). The pill's position for an own-boat fan (trade opts carry no
`seat`, so this is the "ordinary fan" path, not the `onBoats` path `T-013`/`T-017`/`T-235` already
hardened) is chosen by `pillSpotFor(sy, sy)` — no `below` argument, unlike the `onBoats` call three
lines away which passes `belowCircles`. The formation search that places the circles afterward
(`formationOK`, ~3719) DOES treat the already-placed pill as an obstacle, and there IS a final
safety net (~3833–3856): if the circles still land on the pill, the pill itself gets lifted above
the block. **But that lift is clamped: `Math.max(tSafe - 34, blockTop - pillB.height - under - 10)`
— it will never rise above the band's own ceiling.** When the boat is near the top rows on a
390px-wide phone, there is not enough vertical room between the ceiling and the circle block to
fit a 3-line trade pill, so the clamp wins and the pill lands back on the circles anyway. **This is
the exact same failure CLASS already documented and partially fixed for the `onBoats` branch**
(this file's own comment at ~3258–3269, "the two landed on each other with nowhere left to move" —
measured then as 2 of 14 posed fights) — **but here it is unmitigated for the ordinary fan**, and
hits far harder (10 of 14 phone poses, not 2 of 14) because a trade's answer pill runs 3–5 lines
tall against a call's 1–2.

**NOT FIXED THIS WATCH, DELIBERATELY.** This exact function has ~900 lines of hard-won, heavily
commented history (`T-013`, `T-017`, `D-38`, `D-44`, `D-48`, W5-2) precisely because naive fixes
here have repeatedly re-broken something else already paid for. A confident one-line change is

That is real work, sized SMALL-to-MEDIUM, game code, FULL gear, and it is the Chart's to carry
