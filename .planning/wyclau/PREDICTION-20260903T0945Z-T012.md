# PREDICTION — T-012, the downwind battle that ends on a half-sentence

Written 2026-09-03T09:45Z, watch e1 (Wy-Blade), **before any measurement**.

## The claim on the Chart

`judge-1914Z-shots/solo-tablet-wk-018-settled.png` shows a battle card reading

> *"Both fire 🪙 HEADS — but Davy Scones's firing"*

and stopping. The source line (`src/orchestrator.js:712`) writes

> *"Both fire ⚪ HEADS — but ${dwName}'s firing downwind and the shot hits!"*

so **six words are missing from the screen**. Two live explanations, opposite fixes:

- **(A) TIMING** — the screenshot caught a progressive reveal a fraction early. Known Safari settle
  miss; this project's own record has 7 of 27 desktop and 5 of 20 phone screens like it.
- **(B) CLIPPING** — the wrapped second line is cut off by the card, and **every downwind battle in
  the game ends mid-phrase, on every engine.**

## What I expect, and why

**I expect (B) — CLIPPING — is WRONG, and (A) TIMING is also probably wrong. I expect a THIRD
answer nobody on the Chart has named: the string is not clipped and not mid-reveal, it is
TRUNCATED BY THE SCREENSHOT'S OWN CROP or by a card that scrolls.** Reasoning, from reading the
source only:

1. **`battleFooter()` (`src/ui/flow.js:3084`) returns the whole result string in one `innerHTML`
   write.** There is no per-word or per-character reveal inside `.btl-result` — the reveal
   machinery in this project is DOM-order (rule 11: back button → message → buttons → helper), and
   it reveals *elements*, not the characters inside one. So explanation (A) as stated — "a
   progressive reveal caught early" — has no mechanism I can find. If the card is caught early it
   would show the PREVIOUS `battlePublish` state (`"…must answer…"`), not a half of the current one.
2. **That makes the leading suspect CSS on `.btl-result` or `.btl`** — a `max-height`,
   `overflow:hidden`, `-webkit-line-clamp`, or a fixed-height card the second line falls out of.
   That is explanation (B) and it is the one I expect to find IF anything is wrong.
3. **But I lean toward nothing being wrong at all**, because a card that ate the second line of
   every downwind battle would have shown up in a hundred other screens, and Wyatt plays this game.

**So my ranked prediction: (1) not a defect — the second line is present and the crop/judge cut it;
(2) a real CSS clip on WebKit only; (3) timing.** I put (A) as written last because I cannot find
its mechanism.

## The emoji discrepancy — noticed before measuring, flagged not resolved

The screenshot says **🪙** and today's source says **⚪**. Either the screenshot is an older build
(`2026.09.01.7`, which it is) and the copy changed since, or something rewrites the glyph.
`INBOX-20260902T0405Z` records that emoji rendering in the rig is already suspect. **If the glyph
in the shot does not exist in today's source, the shot is evidence about a build we no longer
have**, and that alone changes what this row can conclude.

## WHAT WOULD PROVE ME WRONG

- **Against (1) "not a defect":** a posed downwind battle, photographed after the card has settled,
  whose `.btl-result` element's `scrollHeight` exceeds its `clientHeight`, or whose rendered text
  visibly stops before "and the shot hits!". If I photograph a settled card and the sentence is
  cut, I am wrong and it is a real bug.
- **Against (2) "CSS clip":** `.btl-result` having no height constraint, no `overflow:hidden`, no
  line-clamp, and a measured `scrollHeight === clientHeight` at tablet width in BOTH engines.
- **Against (3):** if the shot's DOM state matches the FINAL `battlePublish` (`winCoin` set, coins
  showing H/H) then nothing was mid-flight and timing is dead.
- **Against my "no mechanism for a partial reveal" reasoning:** if `panel()` or the settle path
  animates text character by character anywhere, my whole first point collapses. **I have not
  checked `panel()`.** That is the specific hole in this prediction and I am naming it before I
  look.

# THE RESULT — written after measuring, 2026-09-03T10:1xZ

## I WAS WRONG ON THE MAIN POINT, AND IT IS THE POINT I WAS MOST CONFIDENT ABOUT

I ranked explanation **A (timing)** LAST, and said so in these words: *"I put (A) as written last
because I cannot find its mechanism."* **A is the answer.** I also named the exact hole that made me
wrong, in the falsifiers, and I was right that it was the hole — just not about where it was:

> *"if `panel()` … animates text character by character anywhere, my whole first point collapses.
> I have not checked `panel()`."*

**The mechanism is not a character-by-character reveal — it is the BOX, not the text.** I reasoned
about how the sentence is written and never about how the container that holds it is sized.
`battleFooter()` does write the whole string at once, which is what I said and it is true; **it is
also irrelevant**, because `#apGrid`'s `grid-template-rows` transitions over 180ms
(`index.html:467`) under `#apGridInner`'s `overflow:hidden` (`:473`). A real broadside publishes the
card twice — `src/orchestrator.js:695` *"<name> loads the cannon…"* (ONE line) then `:712` the
downwind result (TWO lines) — so **the box spends the transition at the OLD one-line height with a
two-line sentence inside it.**
