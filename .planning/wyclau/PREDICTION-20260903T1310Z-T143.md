**MY FALSIFIER FIRED ONCE, AND IT FIRED ON MY OWN INSTRUMENT, NOT ON THE GAME.** I wrote: *"if the
button's painted box and a card's painted box actually intersect... this is a layering bug after
all."* **The first run said exactly that — "4 of 4 award cards intersect the button" — and it was
WRONG.** `getBoundingClientRect()` returns the LAYOUT box, and these cards sit inside a scroller
that CLIPS them, so their boxes run on past the edge while nothing paints there. Had I not written
the falsifier down I would have reported a layering bug, resurrected `T-023`, and contradicted a CEO
who had already opened the pictures and got it right.

**The fix is in the probe and both numbers are now printed side by side** — painted overlap `0px`,
layout-box overlap `30px` — so the difference is in the record instead of being an invisible
correction. *Rule 6: when a check condemns something known to work, suspect the check.*

**AND THE SAME FAULT A SECOND TIME, IN THE SAME FILE, THIRTY LINES DOWN.** The clause that asks
*"which text is sliced through the height of its letters"* reported **0** while the screenshot
plainly showed a half-drawn line. It required a LEAF element, and the line in question — *"Number of
ingredients plundered — 5"* — wraps its number in a `<b>`, so it was never a candidate. **An
instrument that cannot reach its subject reads exactly like a clean screen.** Re-anchored on the box
rather than the leafness: **only 3px of that 34px line is drawn, on both visible cards.**

**WHAT I DID NOT PREDICT, and it is the number that answers his question:** I expected "some content
below the fold". It is **half the screen**. On a 390x664 phone the End of Voyage shows the winner
and TWO of the four award cards; **Dough Hook and Flaky Jack are entirely below the fold (258px
past), and the whole stats table is 454px past it.** The tablet shows all four in one row plus the
complete table. The phone player is not missing a detail; they are missing half the ceremony.

**The trap, named late — I should have written it BEFORE.** I wanted this to be a scroll question
rather than a layering bug, because a layering bug would have meant editing game code while another
watch's sea trial was at sea. That is exactly the motive that makes a wrong first reading
comfortable, and my first reading came out the OTHER way, which is the only reason I noticed.
