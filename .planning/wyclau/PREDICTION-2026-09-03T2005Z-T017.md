the control *"Walk away"* kept its full 9.5px. Exactly the shape predicted.

**2. RIGHT, and it mattered.** `trade_circle_name_fits_check.mjs` passed on the broken build, so a
new check was needed. Its verdict is *"does it fit"*; his ruling is about *at what price*.

grown disc and no arithmetic anywhere needed updating.

label needs, one diameter for every petal, with a ceiling built from the placement's own `SEP`

did not predict at all: a stale cache key that could skip the fit entirely and restore the original
bug.** Found by CEO 184, not by me, and not by either gate — because both gates pose exactly one
fan and the fault needs two in a row.
