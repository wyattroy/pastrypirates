## WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS — THE ASK PILL IS PARKED IN THE CIRCLE'S SEAT, AND THE CULPRIT IS A CONSTANT

pill's bottom plus a hair.** Tablet (2,2): pill `t200..b236`, circle `t244`. Tablet (2,0): pill
`t52..b88`, both circles `t99`. Tablet (9,0): pill `t126..b162`, both circles `t170`. That is

not"* — doing exactly what it says.

**So the circle is not being placed wrong. It is being placed right and then evicted, because the
pill was put where it was going to sit.** `pillSpotFor` (`stage.js:3126-3127`) drops the pill
`bot + R + 34` below the lowest boat in the fight. A circle placed beside that same boat reaches
`bot + rad + HALF + AIR + D/2` below it. Those two numbers are:

| | boat radius | pill drop `R + 34` | circle reach | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **phone**, 35px boats | 19 | **104** | 19+38+6+33 = **96** | clears by 8px — *by luck* |
| **tablet**, 74px boats | 37 | **104** | 37+38+6+33 = **114** | **overlaps by 10px** |
