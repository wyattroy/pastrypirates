## WHAT THE CHART ROW CLAIMS, AND WHY I THINK HALF OF IT IS WRONG

`CHART.md` ⟨`T-142`⟩ states the mechanism as:

> `#pp4Cap` is `position:fixed; left:0; right:0; bottom:0; z-index:22` (`index.html:1748`); modals
> are centred cards at `z-index:1000` **with no scrim over the fixed bar**.

**I predict "with no scrim over the fixed bar" is FALSE**, and that the row's file:line is off by
four lines. Read before measuring:

- `index.html:1752` — not 1748 — is where `#pp4Cap` is declared. 1748 is a comment about the
  tablet bleed rule. The geometry the row quotes is right; the citation is not.
- `index.html:1232` — `.modalOverlay { position: fixed; inset: 0; z-index: 1000; … }`. **That is a
  full-viewport scrim, and it is stacked above `#pp4Cap`'s 22.** So this is NOT a stacking fault
  and NOT a missing scrim.
- The scrim's paint is
  `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 12%, rgba(69,223,166,.22), rgba(41,163,178,.40) 68%)` —
  **22% to 40% alpha.** `#pp4Cap` is `rgba(255,253,242,.97)`, near-opaque cream.

**So my prediction of the real mechanism: the scrim is TRANSLUCENT BY DESIGN, and a near-opaque
cream bar reads straight through a 22–40% teal wash.** Nothing is missing; the covering that exists
was never meant to hide anything, because until the CAPTAINS bar existed there was nothing behind
the scrim but the board, which is exactly what it is meant to let through.

## WHY IT IS TABLET-ONLY — predicted, not measured

`.modalCard` is `max-width:440px; width:100%`, centred by the overlay's flexbox.

- **Phone (≤600px):** the card is ~358px of a 390px viewport. Almost nothing of the bar is left
  uncovered, so the fault has nowhere to show.
- **Desktop:** `body.pp4Stage.pp4Side` moves the panel to a side column
  (`index.html:1634`, `left:calc(100% + var(--pp4CapGap))`), so it is not a bottom bar at all.
- **Tablet (~768–1024px):** the bar is full-bleed (`index.html:1750`,
  `body.pp4Stage:not(.pp4Side).pp4CapBleed #pp4Cap { left:0; right:0; }`) and the card is 440px.
  **That leaves ~160–290px of bar exposed on EACH side, under a wash that hides nothing.**

This also predicts the row's two observed shapes without needing two causes: the recipe modal is
tall and cuts the rows horizontally ("Davy", "Dou" losing their dubloon counts); the End of Voyage
modal is shorter, so the bar shows "out BOTH SIDES". **One bug, one cause, two silhouettes.**
