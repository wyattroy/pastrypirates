<assumptions>
**Placement — stated as an assumption because Wyatt has not ruled on it.** The mute button goes in
`#controlsRow` as a standalone sibling immediately after `#shotClockPanel`, NOT as a third
absolutely-positioned icon on the clock face. Three reasons: AUDIO-02's own wording is "to the right
of the turn clock", which a sibling reads literally, unlike `#scPause` and `#scTimerToggle` which
sit *on* the clock face; `#shotClockPanel`'s only two absolutely-positioned corners are already
taken (`#scPause` top-right at `index.html:398`, `#scTimerToggle` bottom-right at `:407`) and
21-03's D-20 change makes the timer toggle visible in solo for the first time, so a third corner icon
would be brand-new crowding on a panel whose min-width clamps to 70-118px; and `#btnPlayAgain`
already establishes the standalone-sibling-in-`#controlsRow` pattern with a comment saying it is
sized to drop into that slot without the layout jumping. D-16 still falls out for free because
`setClockUI()` sets the sibling's display in the same tick it hides the panel.

**The mute icon in this plan is the bare 🔊 / 🔇 emoji character, not an image.** D-14 asks for a new
speaker icon in the game's style with `blocked-slash.png` over it when muted; that art is a real
dependency and lands in 21-05. Rendering the emoji here means the button's behaviour is complete and
testable now with no broken image and no borrowed icon carrying a second meaning — 🔊 and 🔇 are
deliberately NOT in `EMOJI_IMG`, so `emojify()` leaves them alone. `#scTimerToggle`'s own markup at
`index.html:1052` already ships a literal ⏱️ as its pre-render fallback, so this is the house
pattern, not an invention.
</assumptions>
