    Write the pre-flight checklist into the placeholder section of `19-SAFARI-RUN.md`, then execute
    every item in driven Chrome on a port never used earlier in this session. Rename the driven tab
    to `🤖 CLAUDE IS USING THIS` while in use and rename it back afterwards. Record each item's
    actual observed result inline next to the item — a green tick with no observed value is not a
    result.

    The checklist, in this order:
    1. **A normal build shows nothing.** No query string: `#windHud`, `#windDots` are null and
       `document.querySelectorAll(".wdot").length` is 0.
    2. **The enable link works and needs nothing memorised.** With `?wind=1` the panel appears and
       every control is reachable by pointer alone.
    3. **The dial reaches exactly 0.** Zero `.wdot` elements, and no visible residue anywhere on the
       board.
    4. **The dial reaches exactly 10** — via `#windDial10` in one tap — and exactly 100, never 101.
    5. **The switch is honest.** Turning `#windSwitch` off stops the motion and leaves nothing
       moving on the board; turning it on resumes.
    6. **The dial changes mid-voyage with no reload.** `appState.evIdx` is unchanged across a dial
       change and the same game continues.
    7. **A wind direction change re-aims the dots with no reset.** Advance until the wind changes;
       `.wlayer`'s rotation changes and no dot snaps back to a start position.
    8. **The readout does not lie about backgrounding.** Note the current worst figure, hide the tab
       for about 10 seconds using the page-visibility path, restore it, and confirm the worst figure
       is unchanged while the discarded-pause count went up by at least one. This is the single most
       important item on the list — it is `19-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 3, and it is the failure that
       would falsely fail a build that was fine.
    9. **The baseline is measured, not assumed.** Confirm the readout shows `warming up` before the
       baseline is established and a plain figure afterwards, and that throttling the frame rate in
       devtools produces a baseline matching the throttled rate rather than a permanent stutter
       report. This is Pitfall 2, and it is the failure that would falsely fail Wyatt's phone if it
       happened to be in a power-saving mode.
    10. **The instrument does not contaminate itself.** Compare the reported figure with the panel
        visible against the panel hidden at a dial of 100; a material difference means the readout
        is measuring itself (Pitfall 4).
    11. **Reduced motion behaves.** With reduced motion emulated the dots hold still and stay on
        screen, and the readout still updates.
    12. **The end-of-voyage summary prints.** Finish or force-finish a solo game and confirm
        `#windSummary` appears inside the End of Voyage panel with a typical figure, a worst figure,
        an elapsed figure and the discarded-pause sentence.
    13. **The mechanical gates are green.** `node scripts/wind_dot_contract_check.js` and `npm test`
        both exit 0, with `scripts/fixtures` unchanged.

    If any item is not green, fix it before proceeding — do not carry a known-bad item into Task 2.
    Leave the server running.
  </action>
