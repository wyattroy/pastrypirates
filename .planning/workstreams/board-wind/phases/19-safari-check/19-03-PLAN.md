dot layer over the board → a single shared animation-frame loop that moves the dots and samples
frame timing → an on-screen touch panel carrying the switch, the count control and a live readout →

changing its shape.

    stream in lockstep. That is what keeps a multiplayer room in sync, and it is what 31 saved
