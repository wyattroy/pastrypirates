silent. The derivation and its guard land together.

**The derivation.** Export two pure functions taking only the round number:

- `scentFor(round)` returns `SCENT_CATEGORIES[scentCategoryIndex(round)][Math.floor(round / 7) % 5]`. The line index advances once per full sweep of the categories, so all 35 lines are used exactly once every 35 rounds before any repeats.
