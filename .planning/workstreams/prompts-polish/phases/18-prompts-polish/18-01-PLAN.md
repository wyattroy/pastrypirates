<objective>
Fix the three interlocking narration-panel defects as ONE unit: buttons that appear before the
typewriter has finished (FIX-03), a panel that pins itself shorter than its content after a resize
or rotation (FIX-10), and a fading line that jumps left and gets sliced by an early shrink (FIX-16).

Purpose: these three all live in `panel()`/`resizePanel()` and the panel's single height
measurement. Each one breaks the other two if done alone (ROADMAP Phase 18 design note). They are
planned, executed and verified together, holding BUG-01's measure-once Safari fix intact throughout.

Output: a reveal gate other plans can chain onto, a ghost that fades where it sat, a height floor
shared by the swap path and the resize path, and a resize/orientation listener that re-measures.
</objective>

<hard_constraints>
1. **Measure-once (BUG-01) survives every task in this plan.** `resizePanel()` may do exactly one
   `max-content` reflow-probe per call, and may be called only from: a `panel()` message swap, a
   ghost `drop()`, and a real `resize`/`orientationchange` DOM event. A probe per typewriter tick or
   per animation frame IS the original Safari near-crash.
2. **No `ResizeObserver`/`MutationObserver` on `#apGridInner`.** Its box height is driven by
   `resizePanel()`'s own writes; observing it re-creates the per-frame remeasure loop (RESEARCH
   Pitfall 1).
3. **Nothing in this plan touches `src/engine/index.js`.** `npm test` (which chains
   `determinism_baseline.js --verify`) must stay green.
4. **Do not build a mitigation for late-loading images or web fonts.** RESEARCH confirmed every
   inline panel icon carries `.narrIcon` with fixed `18px`/`18px`, and the project loads no web
   fonts. The confirmed FIX-10 root cause is the resize listener alone.
5. **Line numbers in RESEARCH.md have already drifted** (`.apMsg.fadeOut` is at `index.html:321`,
   not `:308`). Locate every edit site by matching its selector/identifier text, never by line
   number.
