<objective>
Start the shot clock when the buttons become clickable, not when the prompt starts typing — and show
a sane frozen clock during the reveal instead of a ticking or blank one.

Purpose: 18-01 made the buttons wait for the typewriter (D-01). Without this plan a player on the
longest prompts loses up to ~2.8 seconds of a 30 second window (about 14% of the 20s pre-penalty
grace) before they can act. D-02 rules that they get the full window.

Output: a deferred arm chained onto 18-01's reveal gate, a deferred `withShotClock()` so the auto-skip
resolver is still installed, a remote-actor estimate so guests get the same treatment, and a frozen
clock display for the reveal window.
</objective>

<binding_decisions>
- **D-02** — the shot clock starts when the buttons become clickable, NOT at prompt-render. Move
  `armClock(seat)` (`src/ui/util.js:1158`, called from `ask()` at line 1143) to fire from the same
  reveal-completion callback that reveals the buttons. **This overrides RESEARCH.md's Open Question 1
  "leave as-is" recommendation and its Assumption A3 — the research doc is wrong on this point.**
  It carries a UI obligation: `setClockUI()` (`src/ui/panel.js:51`) must show a sane frozen value
  during the 0-2.8s reveal, not a ticking or blank countdown.
- **D-01** — buttons wait for the typewriter; do not re-couple them. The lever for a slow feel is
  `REVEAL_MS_PER_CHAR`.
</binding_decisions>

<hard_constraints>
1. **`withShotClock()` bails out when `seat !== appState.shotClockSeat` and returns `base` unwrapped.**
   That means deferring `armClock()` without also deferring `withShotClock()` silently destroys the
   30 second auto-skip force-resolver for every prompt in the game. This is the single most
   dangerous trap in this plan. Handle it explicitly and prove it with a test.
2. **Only the `ask()` arming site moves.** `src/ui/flow.js:244` (`pickCell`) renders board highlights
   and broadcasts the actor an EMPTY variant — no `.apMsg`, no typewriter, nothing to wait for.
   `src/orchestrator.js:366` is the battle path, which renders `.btlBtn` markup with no `.apMsg` at
   all (`docs/DRIVING-THE-GAME.md` §4d). Both keep arming at prompt time. Do not touch them.
3. **Measure-once (BUG-01) survives.** Nothing in this plan may add a `resizePanel()` call or a
   `max-content` probe. `grep -c 'max-content' src/ui/panel.js` must still return exactly `1`.
4. **Nothing touches `src/engine/index.js`.** `npm test` must stay green.
5. **No new player-facing copy.** The frozen display reuses the exact label/unit/sub strings the
   existing active and waiting branches already render.
6. **No circular import.** `src/ui/panel.js` already imports from `src/ui/util.js`; that direction is
   fine and `armClock` may be added to the existing import list. `src/ui/util.js` must NOT import
   from `src/ui/panel.js`. The two communicate through `appState` fields, following the existing
   `appState.activePickCleanup` precedent (`src/ui/flow.js`).
</hard_constraints>
