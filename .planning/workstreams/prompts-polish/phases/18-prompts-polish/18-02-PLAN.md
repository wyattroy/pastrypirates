<objective>
Make the end-of-voyage win banner print an article only in front of a recipe name that takes one:
"baked a Pound Cake" but "baked Cinnamon-Sugar Churros".

Purpose: the banner currently hardcodes "a" in front of every winning recipe, so eight of the
twenty-one titles read as broken English at the single most celebratory moment in the game.

Output: an explicit per-recipe `article` field, a `recipeArticle()` helper, an automated test that
covers all 21 entries plus the fallback, and one coordinated line change in a file this workstream
does not own.
</objective>
