## Why this plan is not autonomous

D-09 is the phase's blocking approval gate: **Claude drafts the About copy, Wyatt approves it before
it ships, and the phase cannot be marked complete without that sign-off.** Task 2 is a blocking
`checkpoint:decision`. Everything either side of it is unattended.

The gate's scope is wider than the About page alone. `22-UI-SPEC.md`'s Copywriting Contract states
that **every** copy row it carries is a draft pending this sign-off, and CONTEXT.md files the modal's
prompt and confirm wording under Claude's Discretion *"subject to D-09's approval gate."* So one gate
covers all of it, rather than asking Wyatt three separate times.

## Why this plan is wave 3

It edits `about.html` (also touched by Plan 04) and `index.html` (also touched by Plans 01 and 03),
so it cannot share a wave with any of them. It is also genuinely last: there is no point approving
copy before every string that ships exists.
