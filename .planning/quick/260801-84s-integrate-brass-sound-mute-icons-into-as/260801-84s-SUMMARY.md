## Processing

## The one non-obvious decision

**Both icons are cropped to a shared union box, not to their own tight bounding boxes.** Cropped
independently they came out 128×99 and 128×96, putting the horn 3px apart vertically — a visible
twitch every time a toggle flips between them. The union box costs sound-off ~3px of transparent
header and buys exact registration: horn at x2–75, y5–96 in both files, verified by measurement.
This is a deliberate departure from §7's "no buffer space" rule, which is written for single icons,
not toggle pairs. **Any future recrop must preserve it.**

## Verification

Composited at 5× against near-black (the real in-game background) **and** white, plus an alpha map
per §7's explicit warning that a single background colour hides interior keying holes. Result: no
interior holes, no watermark fragment, smooth antialiased edges with a ~1px feather, design
reaching the frame on all four sides. Also rendered at 24px and 48px alongside `stopwatch`,
`anchor`, `gear`, `flip-heads` and `spyglass` — reads as part of the set.

## Deliberately NOT done

**No wiring.** There is no 🔊/🔇 anywhere in `index.html` or `src/`, and no sound, mute, or volume
control exists in the game. These are assets ahead of a feature. Inventing a mute toggle to give
them a home would have been well outside "finish the art process" — when that feature is built,
the icons are waiting.
