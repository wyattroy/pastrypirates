# W4-5 — "Tap and hold the sea to reveal the board"

## What Wyatt asked

> Move the "Tap and hold the sea to reveal the board" tooltip **closer to the recipe card**, and
> **give it the same pulse as the buttons** — *"in a way, it is a button — a button that reveals the
> sea."*

## Measured first, at the moment he means (the recipe picker up)

| | hint top | card top | gap | pulse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| desktop 1200 | 115 | 428 | **295px** | none |
| tablet 768 | 116 | 429 | **295px** | none |
| phone 390 | 140 | 380 | **222px** | none |

Both halves of his complaint are real: it is at the far end of the board from the card, and it does
not pulse.

## THE GRAVEYARD, READ BEFORE TOUCHING IT (rule 10) — this placement is hard-won

`peekHintTick()` in `src/ui/stage.js` does NOT place the hint at a fixed spot, and that is
deliberate. It used to, at `band.bottom - 44`, and the 2026-08-21 gate caught it drawn straight
across "Stay put", across the ✓ of a trade, and over the second line of "Call Flaky Jack" — five
judge findings, one cause. So it became a preference search: **foot → above → head**, each candidate
tested by `clear()` for 6px of air against every control, the question itself, and any narration
box that is talking. If none is clear it hides, because covering the answer is worse than teaching
nothing this tick.

**So the fix is a change of PREFERENCE, not a new fixed position.** Adding a card-adjacent candidate
at the front of that list gets what he asked for while `clear()` still guards every collision the
graveyard records. A hard-coded "put it under the card" would re-open all five findings.

## The two changes

1. **Nearest the card first.** When a visible card is up, the search tries just above it before the
   foot of the board. Derived from the card's own rect, so it is right at any size.
2. **It pulses like a button** — the same `pp4Glow` from the ONE attention-vocabulary block
   (rule 8: same gesture, same behaviour, everywhere), not a second animation that can drift.

## Deliberately NOT changed

The yield mechanism, the 6px air, and the hide-rather-than-cover last resort. Those are what stop
the five judge findings coming back.
