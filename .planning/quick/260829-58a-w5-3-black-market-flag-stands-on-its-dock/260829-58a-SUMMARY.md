# W5-3 — done

> **The black market flags are not attached to the docks. For every dock orientation, set the base of
> the flag on the dock.**

## The cause: two placements for one object

The **dock** is drawn straddling the shared edge with its island — half a cell *toward* it:

```
px = (d[0] + .5 + adj[0]*.5) * cell
```

The **flag** was drawn at the raw dock cell with a hand-picked vertical fraction:

```
x = (fd[0] + .5) * cell,   y = (fd[1] + .42) * cell
```

`adj` is the direction of the island. The dock moves; the flag did not. When a dock happens to face
up the two nearly coincide — which is why it looks fine sometimes — and on the other three
orientations the flag floats half a cell off it. **The `.42` is the tell:** a typed number standing
in for a position the code already computes (rule 9).

## The fix

The dock's placement is computed once and the flag reads it. A `<text>` baseline is the bottom of
the glyph, so putting the baseline at the dock's own centre stands the flag **on** it — his words,
"set the base of the flag on the dock".

## The measurement lied first, and that is worth recording

My first probe recomputed the cell size to derive where the dock *should* be, got it wrong by
**exactly 1.5×**, and reported all seven flags "OFF" — a correct fix looking broken. The tell was the
ratio: flag/dock was 0.667 in *both* axes for *every* dock, which is arithmetic, not a layout fault.
The honest instrument removes my arithmetic entirely and compares the two elements **as the browser
draws them**. Sixth instrument this session that did not reach its subject.
