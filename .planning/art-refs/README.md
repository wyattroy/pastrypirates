# Art references Wyatt has drawn or gathered

**`notes/` is gitignored.** Anything left there is invisible to every machine but the one it was
made on — and to every cloud session, and to every worktree, since a worktree does not share
ignored files. `.planning/art-audit.md` was moved out of `notes/` on 2026-08-02 for exactly this
reason, after ten tracked documents were found pointing at a file nobody else could open. The same
trap caught me on 2026-09-10: Wyatt said *"I already made the crate asset"*, I searched this
worktree, found nothing, and reported it missing. It was in the main checkout's `notes/`.

**So a reference he makes gets COPIED here, in the same turn it is mentioned.**

## crate-reference.png

**57 × 56, RGBA.** His own drawing of the wooden crate frame an ingredient sits inside — a light
timber box, two horizontal slats, open in the middle. Source: `notes/crateref.png` on the Mac.

⚠ **It is a REFERENCE, not a shipped asset**, and the filename he gave it says so. At 57px it
cannot survive a 2× retina screen: the ingredient icons alone already render up to 52px CSS in the
recipe picker, so a crate around one needs roughly 120px of real pixels. The production crate is
GENERATED FROM this, matching its timber, its slat spacing and its proportions — this is the art
direction, not the artwork.

Used for: the captain's box redesign (every ingredient a captain holds) **and the end-game bake-off
crates** — his ruling, 2026-09-10.
