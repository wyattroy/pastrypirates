## ⚠ AND THE CREW-PHONE GUEST — HIS OWN PLAYTEST SEAT — HAS NEVER BEEN A PHONE

Found by comparing two preserved screens element by element (rule 22's discipline applied to
pictures the trial made): `crew-phone-host-012-settled.png` reads **"Tap and hold the sea to reveal
the board"**; `crew-phone-guest-012-settled.png`, same leg, same run, reads **"Click and hold the
sea to reveal the board"**. The host image is 780×1300; the guest image is 390×664.

**Read in the code, not inferred from the pixels:**

- `src/ui/stage.js:547-551` — `holdVerb()` returns *"Tap and hold"* only when
  `matchMedia("(pointer: coarse)")` matches. So the difference is the DEVICE, not host vs guest.
- `scripts/playtest_gate.mjs:358` defines the leg as
  `{ W: 390, H: 664, mobile: true, dsf: 2, guestW: 390, guestH: 664 }`, and its own comment says
  *"Both seats phone-sized, because a crew game between two phones is what he and a friend play."*
- `scripts/playtest_gate.mjs:421-422` opens the **host** with `mobile: !!def.mobile, dsf: def.dsf || 1`.
- **`scripts/playtest_gate.mjs:429` opens the GUEST with neither.** It passes only
  `W, H, dbgPort, httpPort, serveRoot, profileDir`.
- `scripts/lib/cdp.mjs:34` defaults the missing ones: **`mobile = false, dsf = 1`** — and line 70
  only enables touch emulation `if (mobile)`.

**So the crew-phone guest is a 390×664 DESKTOP window at 1×, with a fine pointer and no touch.**
The size was set and the device was not.

**The comment three lines above the bug already says what this costs**, because the identical fault
was found and fixed on the solo phone leg and never swept to the guest — `scripts/lib/cdp.mjs:65-69`:

> *"Measured: the phone leg's screenshot came back reading 'Click and hold the sea' where a real
> phone says 'Tap and hold' (D-40). **A phone leg that does not emulate a phone tests the wrong
> game.**"*

**What it means for the release, stated at the size Wyatt can steer by:**

1. **Crew-on-a-phone is the square he actually playtests** — the leg's own comment says most of his
   35 findings came from it — **and one of its two seats has been a small desktop in every trial
   this project has ever run.** Anything the guest seat proved about tap targets, touch-only
   behaviour, or art at 2× device pixels, it did not prove.
2. **It is a one-line fix and it is NOT this watch's item** — one item per watch, and
   `playtest_gate.mjs` is being read right now by the trial at sea. Chart row written.
3. **It answers a question that has sat open in the record.** `docs/INTENDED-BEHAVIOUR.md:272`
   carries *"The hint 'Click and hold the sea to reveal the board' appeared on a guest and not on a
   host… Observed once, 2026-08-30. **Not measured**"*. It is now measured: **the instrument, not
   the game.** That row can be answered rather than re-wondered a fourth time.

**What would prove me wrong, named before the fix exists** (rule 6's working form): if the guest seat
were being given `mobile`/`dsf` somewhere further down the chain, the guest screenshot would be
