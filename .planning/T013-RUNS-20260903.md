**TWENTY-EIGHT ANCHORED CIRCLES ACROSS TWO RUNS AND NOT ONE OF THEM NAMES THE WRONG CAPTAIN.**
Meanwhile 37 of 56 stranded circles do. A stranded circle is right about a third of the time —
roughly what chance gives you among four hulls.

**AND NO CIRCLE, IN EITHER RUN, EVER LANDED BETWEEN 13px AND 49px.** That hole in the data is the
finding: the placement is not drifting, it is switching. It either puts the circle against the boat
it names, or it throws it somewhere else entirely.

**So "on the wrong boat" is a SYMPTOM, not the fault.** The fault is that the circle is often not
beside any boat at all — which is also what the photograph shows.
