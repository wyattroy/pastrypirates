# Plan 02.2-09 (Group G): the six the judge found

**Source: 46 screenshots from Wyatt's own terminal run of build `2026-08-22b`, judged screen by
screen. Findings and verdicts: `/tmp/gate/judge-findings.txt` (and `judge-results.json` beside it).
The screenshots are in `/tmp/gate/` — OPEN THE ONES NAMED BELOW before you change anything.**

## The six

### 1. PHONE: the battle caption is drawn over the CAPTAINS card — `solo-phone-015.png`, `-016.png`
Two screens, so not a fluke. The ceremony's caption (`.pp4CerSub`, "Tap the coin, captain — let fate
decide.") and the status line set at `4/src/ui/stage.js:1348` ("Crosswind — two heads and the
cannonballs collide.") land on top of the captains card; the captain names read through the white
text and neither is legible. **Desktop is FINE** (`solo-desktop-018.png`, `-019.png`) because the
captains sit in a side column there — so this is a phone/stacked-layout fault, and whatever you do
must leave the desktop rendering alone.
**Clamp the ceremony's text into the board band**, the way every other floating box already is —
`capBandBottom()` is the existing answer for "where does the captains card start". Do not invent a
new number.

### 2. AN OPTION CIRCLE WITH NO NAME — `solo-phone-021.png`
**Diagnosed: `crateOpt()` (`4/src/ui/flow.js:1244`) returns `short: iconImg(ING_IMG[i])` — icon
ONLY, no text.** The radial fan prefers `short`, so a captain's own crates in a GIVE prompt render
as a bare picture while the WANT prompt one screen earlier names every crate
(`solo-phone-011.png`). Same gesture, two behaviours — rule 8.
**Give the short form its name too** (icon + the ingredient's short name), so a circle is never
unlabelled. Check the label still fits the circle at 390px; the fan already has a length rule for
choosing `short`, so keep inside it.

### 3. AN ASK PILL TOUCHING THE CAPTAINS CARD — `solo-phone-023.png`
The pill's bottom edge meets the captains card's top edge with no gap. Everything else on this stage
keeps clear air. The placement code already clamps the pill's TOP into the band; give it the same
respect at the bottom, derived from the gap the neighbouring boxes already use.

### 4. DESKTOP: the recipe picker's subtitle is sliced — `solo-desktop-004.png`, `-005.png`
*"Tap a recipe to highlight its docks"* (`.pp4RecipeHint`) is cut across by the top edge of the two
recipe cards. **`-005` is a settled state — the "Bake this!" overlay is up — so this is NOT an
animation artifact.** The phone is fine (`solo-phone-005.png`), so it is the desktop/wide layout of
this modal. Find why the cards overlap the line and give it its own room.

### 5. DESKTOP: a narration bubble UNDER the sail squares — `solo-desktop-014.png`
Only the fragments "Ahoy" and "turn!" show between the yellow squares; the rest of the sentence is
hidden behind them. **This is the inverse of D-38** — that rule says nothing may cover a sail square;
here the squares cover the words.
**MEASURE THE STACKING BEFORE YOU CHANGE IT.** On paper `#sailHost` is `z-index:2` inside
`#boardwrap` while `.pp4Bub` lives in `#pp4Fx` at `z-index:21`, so the bubble SHOULD already win —
which means the real cause is a stacking context, not a z-index, and guessing at it will produce a
fix that works on one screen and not the next. Read the computed stacking on the real page first and
say in the summary what you found. **D-38's own rule decides the outcome: the bubble must not cover
a sail square, and a sail square must not make the words unreadable — if both cannot hold, the
BUBBLE moves** (it has a placement search; the squares do not).

### 6. BATTLE CIRCLES TOO TIGHT — `solo-desktop-020.png`
"Fire again" and "Break off" sit ~10px apart, tighter than the derived `D/4` gap Group F gave the
ordinary fan. Battle prompts use `.btlBtn`, not `.apBtn` — check whether they go through the same
placement at all. If they do not, that is the finding: **one gap rule, reached from both**, rather
than a second number.
