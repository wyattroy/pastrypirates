**Reproducibility:** measured on three independent runs. The 27-char hold came out 1828 / 1831 /
1827 ms — a 4 ms spread across separate browser sessions.

**THE PLAN'S OWN TASK 3 ASSERTION IS SUPERSEDED AND WAS NOT SATISFIED.** It required
`Math.abs(A.long - B.long) > 400` to throw — the D-10 ceiling must not move. **D-45 post-dates the

15 % faster, long ~5.3 s → ~4.5 s. The assertion is superseded, not evaded. D-10's *intent* (a long
line stays long enough to read) is untouched; only its *number* moved, in the open, by the person
who made both rulings.

**The honest shape of the change, in his terms:** short lines — the turn banners and flip results a
player reads most often — get about **0.7 s back**. Lines in the 70–85 character band get about
**1 s longer**, because that is the curve he picked against two anchors. Everything past ~85

## 8. ⚠ THE ONE ITEM I DO NOT BELIEVE IS SOLVED — his item 1 (D-32)

**This is the row to read.** The shared pulse shipped, it is defined once, it reuses `.sailCell`'s
own keyframes, and it carries the reduced-motion opt-out. **But it is not proven to reach the
buttons he was complaining about, and the measurement suggests it may not matter if it does.**

notice" is about buttons that were already pulsing, and a pulse is not the answer.

### ⚠ THE CREW LEG DID NOT COMPLETE, and PAR-02's guest question is NOT answered by this run

Two real Chromes, two real seats, a real Firebase room (codes `JTRW` then `BNXM`) — the host and
guest both reached the lobby. **The voyage never started:** `btnStart` never became visible inside
45 s, so no stage was built on either tier.

**The rig's own red-proof is what caught it, working exactly as designed.** The visibility probe was
armed against two knowns — `#pp4FF`, hidden by design in a crew game (D-04), and `#pp4Menu`, always
shown once the stage exists. It reported `exists: false` for **both**, on **both** tiers. A
predicate that says "missing" for the thing that is always there is not measuring a broken guest; it
is measuring a **lobby**. Had that red-proof not been there, this run would have reported "the guest
does not draw the wind pill" — a phantom PAR-02 failure, from a probe pointed at the wrong screen.
That is precisely the class of mistake that cost this project two days earlier in the month.

**So, stated plainly rather than dressed up:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Established | the rig creates a room and both clients join it |
| **NOT established** | **whether a guest draws the wind pill (PAR-02)** |
| **NOT established** | the storm summary and the Dock petal as rendered on a guest |
| Why it stopped | the lobby needs enough captains before `btnStart` unhides (`lobby.js:382`); adding bots to the room was rig work, not game work, and the night's budget went to the game |

**What is known from the code instead, and it is an argument, not a measurement:** `pillTick()` is
called from the single stage frame loop at `stage.js:2493` that every client runs — one path, not
