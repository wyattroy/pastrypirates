# Mouse-driven QA on build 2026-08-20t — three complete solo voyages, three desktop sizes

**Why this exists.** Wyatt, 2026-08-21 7am, after his playtest found a game-stopping fault (the Dock
button off-screen on desktop) that the overnight headless QA had sailed past: *"did you actually
play the game actually in a browser actually by using a mouse and actually watching the screen?"*
The overnight driver clicked DOM nodes; an off-screen button is still clickable to a script.

**The instrument (scratchpad `mouse_qa.mjs`, real Chrome over CDP):** every click is a trusted
`Input.dispatchMouseEvent` at screen coordinates, allowed ONLY if the target is inside the viewport,
inside `body`'s rendered column, and is what `elementFromPoint` returns at its centre. A control
that exists but fails that gate for ≥4 consecutive ticks (≥12 for zero-size, which intros and card
animations produce legitimately) is a FINDING, never a click. Screenshot per action; a full shot plus
a 2× close-up the instant a new subject narration bubble (`.pp4Bub` with `.pp4Tail`) appears.
One run was HEADED with a drawn cursor so Wyatt could watch.
