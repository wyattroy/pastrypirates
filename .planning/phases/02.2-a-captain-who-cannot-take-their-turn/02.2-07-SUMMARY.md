## Rule 23 — where two paths became one, and where a seam remains

**Converged:** the once-per-voyage ceremony gate. `eventCeremony()` in `util.js` is now the single
gate that `narrateLastEvent()` (human) and `narrateCurrent()` (bot) both call. The next feature keyed
on a once-per-voyage stamp gets both paths for free instead of paying for this again. The edge
crosses through `handlers.js` — the seam that already carries `onFlash` and `onLiveRender` — because
`util.js` is imported *by* `panel.js` and can never import it back.

**Converged:** the narration hold. One `narrationHoldMs()`, read by the stage bubble and by
`panel.js`'s classic-path fallback, so the two cannot drift on how long a line reads.

**SEAM CARRIED FORWARD, named rather than left looking converged:** `narrateLastEvent()` and
`narrateCurrent()` are still **two functions**. Only their ceremony gate was unified. They still
differ in what they read (the last event vs `logLines[evIdx]`) and in how they pick a variant.
Converging them fully reaches into the layering rules and is a bigger change than Group E.
