## The solo constraint — CONFIRMED BY READING, not taken on trust

This is the single assumption the whole staging rests on. It holds.

1. **`runLiveNet()` runs in solo and pass-and-play.** `beginGame()` `:1656` forks on `appState.isHost`, and `isHost` is true in solo — there is no room at all. The `else` branch (the nine watchers) never runs there.
2. **Chat is switched off entirely in solo**, which is why `watchChat` gets away with the pure-loopback shape: `beginGame()` `:1647` sets `$("chatPanel").style.display=(appState.db&&appState.room)?"":"none"`, and `sendChat()` `:314` opens with `if(!appState.db||!appState.room)return`. **The game display cannot be switched off.**
3. **The net writers dereference `db` with no null guard.** `4/src/net/writers.js` — every writer is unconditional by design (see its own header: *"The host-authority and mid-replay-suppression checks sit ahead of most of these writes at their call sites. They stay there."*). `netSetPrompt` `:71` is the plan's named example and it is accurate.
4. **The guards live at the call sites, and they already have the right shape.** `netNarrate` `:304`: `if(appState.isHost&&appState.db&&appState.room)netSetNarr(...)`. `pushEvents` `:1136`: `if(!appState.db||!appState.room)return`. `broadcastClock` `:159`: same.
5. **`remotePrompt` has NO `db` guard — and is unreachable in solo.** `ask()` (`ui/util.js:1554`) only reaches it when `decisionIsLocal(seat)` is false, and `decisionIsLocal` returns **true for any human seat at a pass-and-play table** (stated in `ui/util.js`'s own comment above `ask()`). In solo every human seat is the local seat. So the unguarded writer is protected by the fork above it, not by a guard of its own — **which is exactly why Stage 4 is the dangerous one: it moves that fork.**

### What every stage must do when `db` and `room` are absent

> **The host feeds the LOCAL dispatch and draws from it synchronously. Firebase is mirrored to only when `db && room`. There is never a round trip on the host's own screen — in any mode.**

That is the same `isHost && db && room` shape the guards above already use, so no stage introduces a new guard pattern. A two-tab check **cannot** see a violation of this by construction, because a two-tab check always has a room. **Every stage must be played solo.**
