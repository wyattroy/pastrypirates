**This is the one item in the phase that touches the engine, and that is why it is its own plan.**
The engine is seeded and lockstep replay depends on it: changing what it emits into the event stream
invalidates the determinism corpus. **The corpus does not exist yet — Phase 3 (TEST-03) captures
it — so the cost today is low, but the change is flagged explicitly rather than slipped in beside
render fixes.** After Phase 3 records the corpus this door closes; doing item 4 now is doing it at
the only cheap moment.

**Wyatt's ruling (D-15): the bot BAKES.** A bot docking at Tortuga with a full recipe fires the ovens
and forfeits the pass dubloon, because that is what a human would obviously do. This restores the
standing bots-and-humans parity invariant: **if a human cannot do a thing, a bot must not either.**

Purpose: bots play by the same rules as people.
Output: one guard, in two places, and an automated proof from the event stream.
</objective>

  <action>
    **CONFIRMED, exact mechanism, in TWO separate sites that must both be fixed.**

    `lightOvens(p)` at `4/src/engine/index.js:2770-2771` fires **unconditionally** whenever the
    bake-off is on and the captain can bake and is not already baking or done — regardless of what
    action that captain took this turn. The day loop calls it once per captain, right after the turn
    resolves.

    **For a human the collision is already prevented.** `4/src/ui/flow.js:1795` computes `canOvens`
    from the same `canBake(p)`; when it is true the menu shows "Fire up the ovens!" and the Pass
    button is **suppressed entirely** (`4/src/ui/flow.js:1832` pushes Pass only when `!canOvens`). A
    human physically cannot collect the pass dubloon on the turn they become bake-eligible.

    **For a bot there is no equivalent guard, in either of the two places a bot's turn is decided:**
    1. `takeTurn(p, windDir, storm)` — `4/src/engine/index.js:2742`. Its fallback ends `this.doPass(p)`
       with no bake check at all. This is the **headless** path.
    2. `botTurn(p)` — `4/src/ui/flow.js:2170`. Its fallback ends `g.doPass(p)` with no bake check
       either. This is the path **a real browser game actually runs** for a bot's turn.

    **Both must land together, and the code itself says why.** The comment at
    `4/src/ui/flow.js:2159-2162` is explicit: *"botTurn does not call Game.takeTurn — it reimplements
    the turn so each step can animate… A fallback added only to the engine would fix the simulator
    and leave every real browser game exactly as broken, which is the opposite of the point."*

    Add the bake-eligibility guard mirroring `4/src/ui/flow.js:1795`'s `canOvens` before **each** of
    those two `doPass` calls, so a bake-eligible bot ends its turn silently and the ovens light from
    the day loop's existing unconditional call. **Reuse `canBake(p)` — do not write a second
    eligibility test.** The shared engine method is what makes "same rule for bots and humans" a
    property of the code rather than a claim about two sites that happen to agree today, which is
    exactly what the comment above the existing `doPass` in `botTurn` already says.

    **ENGINE-EMISSION CHANGE — say it out loud in the summary.** The `4/src/engine/index.js:2742`
    guard changes what `takeTurn()` emits: a pass event that fires today in this exact scenario stops
    firing. That is a change to the event stream, which the headless simulator and any future
    determinism replay read. Per `docs/DETERMINISM-RERECORD.md` the corpus does not exist yet
    (Phase 3 captures it), **so this costs nothing today** — but it is an engine-tier change, not a
    UI-tier one, and the record must say so. The `4/src/ui/flow.js:2170` half is pure UI-tier and
    carries no such cost by itself.

    **Do not fix this with a constant.** No turn counters, no "if it is late enough in the voyage"
    thresholds. The condition is `canBake(p)` and nothing else — the same condition the human path
    already uses. **A bot's first crate and its last crate are not the same trade, and a hardcoded
    threshold cannot be right for both.**
  </action>
