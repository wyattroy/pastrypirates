<objective>
Make the bot's hail obey the rule humans already follow: sail, then spend your one action. Give it a
deliberate target and a deal worth taking, and make the cost of a refused hail visible.

Purpose: AI-01's reported symptom — "Flaky Jack offered 5 for Wyyyy's Speckled Eggs — refused" followed
by "Flaky Jack casts a line" — is one bot taking two actions in a turn. The governing principle (D-01)
is that bots follow the same rules as humans AND the player can see that they do; a hail that costs
nothing is the most visible violation of that in the game.

Output: three pure, unit-tested helpers, a restructured hail block that ends the bot's turn, and a
closing narration line that shows the price being paid.
</objective>

deliberately keeps it out of the engine's action model — `chooseAction` gains no branch and the

<planner_corrections>
**RESEARCH.md assumption A2 is wrong as written and must not be implemented literally.** RESEARCH
proposes proxying D-06's "whoever it hurts least" by "prefer the holder whose `needs(q)` does NOT
include `ing`". `needs(p)` is `p.recipe.filter(i=>!p.ing.includes(i))` (`src/engine/index.js:239`),
so for any player who *holds* `ing`, `ing` is never in `needs(q)` — the proxy is constant-false and
ranks nothing. The correct proxy, and the one the codebase already uses for exactly this judgement,
is `humanTrade`'s `essential` idiom at `src/ui/flow.js:370`: `q.recipe.includes(ing) && g.cnt(q.ing,ing) <= 1`.
Use that. This correction is carried into the task action below.
</planner_corrections>
