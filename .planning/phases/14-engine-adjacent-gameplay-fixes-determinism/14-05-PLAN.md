<objective>
Make a storm readable. The boat steps square by square, the board keeps up with the words, every
outcome gets said out loud for bots as well as humans, and the "dock held fast" line finally tells you
which of three things actually happened.

Purpose: today two `windPush` calls fire back to back with no render and one narration beat between
them (`src/ui/flow.js:559-566`), so a bot teleports up to four squares while at most one of its
outcomes is ever narrated. On the human side the board does not redraw on ordinary water
(`src/ui/flow.js:255-262`), so an outcome line plays while the boat is still shown a square short —
which is the reported "false dock held fast" symptom (D-22). D-01's governing principle is that the
player must be able to SEE that bots play by the same rules.

Output: a per-square human push, a narrating per-square bot leg, bot-paced narration, and three honest
`moored` lines.
</objective>
