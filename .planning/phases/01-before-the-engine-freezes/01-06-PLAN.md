**No constant may be introduced to settle this** (CLAUDE.md §2, `docs/BOT-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md`
principle 10). A hardcoded "material" threshold is a price list standing in for a quantity that moves
by an order of magnitude across a voyage; it cannot be right for a bot's first crate and its last,
and it fails silently. The elegant version almost always deletes code — when a bot needed a trade
priced properly, the answer was `acquireTurns()`, something already being computed, and the whole
test collapsed to one comparison with no threshold in it. Here the equivalent move is: report the
movement next to the game's own in-run spread and ask the person whose call it is.
</context>
