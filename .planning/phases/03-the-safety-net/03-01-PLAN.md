## 1. THE ONE-WAY DOOR — why TEST-03 is NOT in this plan. Read this before anything else.

**This is a judgement, made explicitly, and it can be overruled.** ROADMAP's Phase 3 says capture the
corpus here. This plan says: capture it LAST, in its own plan, and preferably after Phases 4 and 5
have been scoped. Here is the whole argument so it can be argued with.

**What the door costs.** `docs/DETERMINISM-RERECORD.md` and `-NEXT.md` §7: any change to what
`4/src/engine/index.js` emits into the event stream — *including adding a field to an existing
event* — invalidates every fixture and forces a gated re-record: a full per-seed attributed
divergence report, a blocking human decision on it, then one capture run. `02.2-04` already made an
engine-emission change and disclosed it as free *"since the determinism corpus does not exist yet
(Phase 3/TEST-03 captures it), but the door closes the moment it does."* **That disclosure was
correct and it is still true today: `4/scripts/fixtures/` does not exist.**

**Why capturing tonight is the wrong trade, in three measured points:**

1. **The corpus is an oracle against UNINTENDED engine drift. Phases 4 and 5 are the two phases most
   likely to make INTENDED engine changes.** A gate that fires on changes you meant to make is
   `docs/HARD-WON-LESSONS.md` §9 exactly: *"A GATE THAT FIRES ON EVERY SCREEN TRAINS ITS READER TO
   IGNORE IT — WHICH IS WORSE THAN NO GATE."*

2. **The claim that Phases 4 and 5 will not need an engine change is an INFERENCE, not a
   measurement** — and CLAUDE.md rule 6 forbids acting on the first as if it were the second.
   ROADMAP sources it to intake research written 2026-08-18 against a tree that has since been
   changed by 02.2-04 (an engine change) and rewired by 02.15 (the whole orchestration). What HAS
   been measured, on 2026-08-22 (D-53, four real Firebase rooms): *"a guest CANNOT take their own
   bake — there is no channel to send it down."* Building that channel is Phase 4's first criterion,
   and nobody has yet measured whether it can be built without the engine.

3. **The purity fixes queued in `docs/DETERMINISM-RERECORD-NEXT.md` are inherited by `4/` — CONFIRMED
   IN CODE TONIGHT, not assumed** — and one of them sits on the event Phase 5 exists to change:

   | Queued fix | Where it lives in `4/` |
   |---|---|
   | `spoil` carries rendered text | `4/src/engine/index.js:1793`, emitted `:1797` |
   | `gave` carries rendered text | `4/src/engine/index.js:1140` via `offerLabel()` at `:1166` |
   | `ilabelImg` imported into the engine tier | `4/src/engine/index.js:8` |

   `gave` is on the **trade** event. **Phase 5 is trade over the wire.** Freezing a prose-carrying
   trade field the night before the trade phase is the most expensive possible moment to spend the
   door.

**And the UI half is not small.** `-NEXT.md` §6 is explicit that the interim display-layer fix must be
REMOVED, not left beside the new path. In `4/` that interim fix is roughly **fifteen sites in
`4/src/ui/util.js`** (`:804`, `:817`, `:831`, `:845`, `:851`, `:854`, `:868`, `:871`, `:915`, `:917`,

and `parseInt(e.spoil,10)`. That is a careful pass over battle and trade narration — the exact copy
Wyatt has been iterating on all week — and it must not ride along beside five gate ports.

**So the ruling this plan takes:** TEST-03 becomes **plan 03-03**, and it lands the three purity
fixes and the capture as ONE pass, exactly as `-NEXT.md` §7 demands (*"one re-record, not three"*).
**Task 5 writes `docs/DETERMINISM-CAPTURE-4.md` tonight** so the procedure exists, is costed, and is

**THE ALTERNATIVE, RECORDED SO IT CAN BE CHOSEN INSTEAD:** capture tonight and hold Phases 4 and 5 to

this plan refuses to do is take it BY DEFAULT, at 2am, without him.

## 3. THE RED-PROOF PROTOCOL — identical for every gate in this plan. Non-negotiable.

`docs/HARD-WON-LESSONS.md` §3: *"a gate scanning the wrong tree is not silent, it is reassuring"* —
and §2: *"a check you have only ever seen pass is indistinguishable from a check that cannot fail."*

**Both halves are required. A one-sided red-proof proves nothing about the AIM.**

1. Run the gate against `4/`. Record the verdict verbatim.
2. **Plant a deliberate fault in a `4/` file the gate claims to cover** — a real one, of the class the
   assertion exists to catch, not a syntax error.
3. Run BOTH aims:
   - `--tree=4` **must go RED**, naming the planted fault.
   - the bare root run **must stay GREEN** — this is the half that proves the two aims are actually
     different and that the root suite could never have caught it.
4. Revert the fault. Prove the revert as a command, do not assert it:
   `git diff --name-only -- 4/` must print nothing.
5. Re-run both aims and record the restored verdicts.

**Paste the RED output into `03-01-SUMMARY.md`, one block per gate.** A summary that says "red-proofed"
without the red output in it is a claim, not evidence.

**Never weaken an assertion to make it green.** If an assertion cannot be made honest against `4/`
tonight, leave it out of the blocking chain and write down why — that is what Task 5 is for.
