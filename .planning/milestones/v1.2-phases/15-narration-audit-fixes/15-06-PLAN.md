<hard_constraint>
`src/engine/index.js` stays READ-ONLY. A cut or merge is a narration-layer change only: an event type whose line is cut still fires and is still recorded — only its text stops rendering. Do NOT remove an event, a field, or a `reason` value to implement a cut. The 31 determinism fixtures serialise those fields.

`EVENT_NARRATION.moored`'s third-person strings are asserted by `scripts/bot_storm_narration_test.js`, which is a permanent gate and must keep passing. If Wyatt's marks change `moored`'s wording, update that gate's literal expectations in the same commit — do not weaken the assertions to accommodate the new text, and do not leave the gate red.
</hard_constraint>

<authority_rule>
`.planning/phases/15-narration-audit-fixes/15-COPY-APPROVED.md` is the only authority for what changes in this plan.

- A row marked **keep** means the line ships exactly as it stands. Do not "improve" it.
- A row marked **cut** means the line stops rendering. Remove the string and its call site, leaving the event and any state bookkeeping intact.
- A row marked **merge** names a partner. Collapse the two onto the partner's text at the narration layer only, the way `moored`'s `home` case was collapsed onto `justDocked` in Phase 14 — the engine's own distinction is untouched.
- A row marked **rewrite** ships that row's final text verbatim, including its punctuation and emoji.

If a row is ambiguous, contradicts another row, or names a line that no longer exists, STOP and surface it rather than choosing. A disagreement with the approved record is raised, never resolved unilaterally.
</authority_rule>
