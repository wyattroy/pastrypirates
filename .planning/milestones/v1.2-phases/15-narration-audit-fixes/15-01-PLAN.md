<assumption_delta_decision>
The assumption-delta detector fired (kind `pluralization`) on this phase. The literal trigger term was a coincidence, but the underlying signal is real.

- **Noun now primary:** a narration message is no longer "the rendered HTML string". It is *a narration message with per-viewer renderings* — one viewer-neutral default plus zero or more seat-addressed variants.
- **Decision:** `add-alongside`. `html` stays the primary field on `rooms/{code}/narr`; `variants` is added beside it. Rationale: version-skew safety — a mid-game client on either side of the change must degrade to today's text rather than a blank box (D-10's explicit requirement), and only an additive field gives that in both directions.
- **Accepted debt:** two representations of "what this message says" now coexist on one payload. What would force a later promote to a single per-viewer model: a narration line whose *content* (not just its address) genuinely differs per seat, or a second consumer of the payload that cannot be taught the fallback rule.
- **Suggested (not required) invariant test:** every narration message renders correctly for every viewer seat, including a viewer with no seat assigned. The `pickNarrVariant` assertions in Task 1/2 are the first instance of it.
</assumption_delta_decision>
