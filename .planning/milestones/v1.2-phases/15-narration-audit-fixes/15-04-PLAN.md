<research_correction>
**CONTEXT.md's D-12 cites a string that never appears in a real game.** The `"N coins (all they had)"` form is produced only by `Game.battle()` in `src/engine/index.js`, which is called exclusively by the offline determinism simulator. Every real game — solo, pass-and-play, online, human or bot — resolves battle spoils through `asyncBattle()` in `src/orchestrator.js:377-548`, whose coin spoil string is the take plus " coins" with no suffix, ever.

**Do not match on any substring.** Both paths clamp the coin take to at most 5, so the split is derived from the leading number: when no ingredient changed hands and that number is 5, the loser had a full purse and chose to pay — a genuine bribe. When it is below 5, the loser had fewer than 5 coins and (on the live path) necessarily zero crates, because holding a crate would have routed to the ingredient branch instead. That is the cleaned-out case, and it is what produces the small-coin line Wyatt reported.

The conclusion D-12 reaches is correct and achievable with zero engine change; only its premise about the string needed correcting.
</research_correction>
