<objective>
Produce the NARR-01 deliverable — a browser page that renders every narration line the way a player experiences it, tagged with a pruning recommendation and paired with the proposed new wording — and take it to Wyatt as the phase's single review pass.

Implements **D-01** (a browser page, not a document), **D-02** (every line tagged keep / cut / merge / rewrite), **D-03** (both narration sources, or the audit is incomplete), **D-04** (one review pass covering pruning marks and proposed wording side by side), and **D-06** (bias toward rewriting, not deleting).

Purpose: repetition has to be felt the way a player experiences it. Raw strings with placeholders and emoji-as-code hide exactly the sameness this audit exists to surface — which is why this is a rendered page and not a list.
Output: an extraction script, a generated inventory, the review page, and Wyatt's recorded dispositions.
</objective>
