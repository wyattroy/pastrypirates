<objective>
Establish the entire de-globalization pipeline and prove it end-to-end on ONE representative reassigned name (`room`) before migrating the other 45. This is the tracer slice: build the shared tokenizer, the migration tool, the app-state module, and the standing contract check; wire `state` into the existing PP bridge; migrate `room` at every site; then gate the mechanism in a real browser before any bulk migration proceeds.

Purpose: The real difficulty of this phase is not the 40 closures (a paper tiger per D-01) — it is that ~1,269 bare-identifier occurrences of 46 genuinely mutable, reassigned names are scattered through one 3,809-line classic `<script>` block, and a naive text migration provably corrupts real string literals in this exact file. Prove the mechanism once, cheaply, before betting 45 more names on it.

Output: The tokenizer, the migration tool, `src/state/index.js`, `scripts/state_contract_check.js`, the bridge wiring, and a fully-migrated `room` — validated in-browser.
</objective>
