## Resolution

root_cause: |
  ONE mechanism drives cases A, C, D (measured) and B (code-derived, same call path): `ask()` (4/src/ui/util.js:1656) unconditionally posts a `.pp4Bub` "wait line" narration bubble carrying the SAME text as the prompt about to be built, via a call path (`showNarration()` → `window.__pp4.narr()` → `stageFlash()`) that item 11's `pendingReveal`/`stageSettled()` gate never covers. In the SAME synchronous JS turn, `localAsk()` builds the real prompt via `panel()`, whose trailing `syncPrompt()` call runs `promptTick()` synchronously — and `promptTick()`'s item-19 wait-line-retirement check (4/src/ui/stage.js:1513) sees the panel go empty→non-empty and retires WHATEVER wait line is currently registered, including the one `ask()` itself posted a couple of milliseconds earlier for this very prompt. Result: the bubble is created and marked for its 300ms fade within 0-2ms of appearing — a visible "pop and immediately vanish" — while the real, gated prompt panel stays hidden for a further 700ms-1.2s+ (bounded by `SETTLE_CAP_MS`=1400ms) before it actually reveals. Two ADDITIONAL, separate findings specific to the dock-flip case: (1) the flip-result bubble ("X flips TAILS") sits behind the full-screen `#pp4Veil` (z-index 44, 4/index.html:1733) for its first ~1.2s because `.pp4Bub`'s stacking context `#pp4Fx` is z-index 21 (4/index.html:1721) — so ~47% of its 2550ms hold floor is wasted invisible; (2) the Buy-prompt pill's ~63px right-edge overhang at 1400px is a SEPARATE bug, leading (unconfirmed) hypothesis: a stale/early `msg.offsetWidth` read at stage.js:1715 that does not account for an inline ingredient-icon `<img>` decoding later or a `radKey` (stage.js:1708-1710) that doesn't key on the message's own content.
