01

Step 5 is not done. I stopped, and I want you to check my reasoning.

You asked for the Decider — one way of asking who answers for a seat, so the game stops branching on which mode you are in. I went to build it, found most of the machinery already there, and talked myself into calling it finished. A fresh reviewer took that apart, and it was right.

What is genuinely already there: the game asks two separate questions — is this seat a person or a bot? and is that person at this device? Across every mode they agree six times out of seven and differ in exactly one case: you are hosting and it is a remote friend's turn. A person answers, but not on your screen. That one row is why both questions exist, and merging them would break it.

What I wrongly claimed was covered: thirteen places still ask "are we in pass-and-play?", and at least three of them decide what gets drawn — whether a recipe card can be revealed, whether the check-my-recipe button appears, whether the bots-thinking indicator shows. Those are exactly what the Decider is meant to absorb, and none of them moved.

And the check I built instead could not fail. I wrote a gate to lock the seven-row table, but it had the rule typed into it rather than running the real one — so changing the real rule left the gate green. The reviewer broke it in one line. It is fixed now: the rule was pulled out into the pure layer so the gate runs the same function the game runs, and I re-broke it twice more to prove it catches them.
